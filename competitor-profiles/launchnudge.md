## Positioning and Messaging

LaunchNudge focuses on indie developers who dislike launch marketing. It frames the problem as hours wasted writing platform posts that get no traction, then promises repo/changelog-to-platform-post generation.

## Strengths

- Strong pain framing for solo developers and bootstrappers.
- Multi-channel launch focus.
- GitHub/changelog input overlaps with QuickFork.
- Clear platform-specific output framing.

## Weaknesses / Gaps

- Very traction/virality oriented, which can trigger developer skepticism.
- No visible README-card, social preview image, source identity, QA, manifest, or multilingual artifact package in quick scan.
- Some testimonial-style proof appears informal and should be independently validated before relying on it.

## Implications for QuickFork

LaunchNudge validates that "repo to launch materials" is a live category. QuickFork should stay more credible and artifact-centered: launch-ready assets, not viral-post promises.
