## Market Gaps

1. **Trust gap:** Many tools say AI or automation, but few make the evidence trail visible.
2. **Artifact gap:** README tools stop at markdown; image tools stop at visuals; launch tools stop at posts.
3. **Open-source fit gap:** Developer audiences resist generic launch marketing and README clutter.
4. **Localization gap:** Competitor scans rarely mention aligned multilingual launch output.
5. **QA gap:** Competitors do not foreground checks for invented metrics, wrong logos, or locale slot drift.

## Strategic Implications for QuickFork

- Lead with "source-backed repo-to-launch package," not "AI README generator."
- Treat README, social preview, and launch post surfaces as connected outputs.
- Make the artifact trail visible: curated brief, prompt, image, QA report, manifest.
- Keep developer trust central: no invented metrics, no random logos, no automatic publishing.
- Use public showcase pages as proof instead of generic testimonials until real customer proof exists.
